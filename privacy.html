The essentials

Meyaya processes data to power its features. AI requests can reach external providers; some memories and game records persist. You can inspect saved memories and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion.

01Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayaya (the “operator,” “we,” or “us”) processes information when you use Meyaya, a Discord bot for AI conversation, social interactions, fantasy games, profiles, moderation, and voice features. It also covers these public policy pages.

Discord operates its platform under its own Privacy Policy. This policy describes Meyaya’s practices and does not replace the policies of Discord or other providers.

02Information Meyaya processes

CategoryExamples and purpose
Identity & contextDiscord user, server, channel, and message IDs; display names, avatars, profile information, roles, permissions, and timestamps, where available and relevant to a feature.
Messages & AI requestsCommands, prompts, replies, relevant conversation context, and attachments or links submitted for processing. Enabled chat, roleplay, monitoring, or moderation features may also process accessible channel messages. Context can include a message you reply to.
Saved memoriesStructured facts, preferences, conversation summaries, nicknames, relationship state, source references, and confidence or conflict information used for continuity and personalization.
Games & social featuresCharacters, weapons, abilities, guardians, progression, runs, results, collections, interaction counts, relationship pairs, and fictional marriage records.
VoiceAudio from participating voice channels while the bot is connected and listening, relevant conversational text, and generated audio. Audio is streamed to the AI service to understand and respond.
OperationsServer settings, permission snapshots, moderation state, usage counts, captured requests and replies, errors, performance timings, and other service diagnostics.

Not every feature uses every category. Records may concern members mentioned in commands or participating in enabled server features even if they did not invoke Meyaya directly. Avoid submitting credentials, financial information, identity documents, or other sensitive information.

03How information is used

  • Run commands, generate responses and profile images, maintain game progress, and operate social features.
  • Personalize conversations using relevant context and saved memories.
  • Provide requested AI, media, search, and voice functions.
  • Apply server settings, permissions, safety controls, rate limits, and moderation features.
  • Diagnose failures, measure service performance and usage, prevent abuse, and respond to support or data requests.

We do not sell personal information or use it to build advertising profiles.

04Providers & other recipients

Information needed to deliver a feature may be transmitted to the following recipients:

  • Discord: messages, commands, interactions, files, and voice communication delivered through its platform.
  • Google Gemini: prompts, relevant conversation history, saved context, submitted media, and voice audio used by AI features. Requests can contain Discord names or identifiers included in context.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): infrastructure hosting Meyaya and processing its service traffic and operational data.
  • Database and cache infrastructure: PostgreSQL and Redis services store persistent feature records and temporary state. These may run with the bot or through managed infrastructure providers, including Neon for PostgreSQL and Upstash for Redis when configured.
  • Feature-specific providers: for example, Klipy for GIF search, AniList for character information, and search or linked-media services needed for a requested feature. Search terms, requested URLs, network metadata, and other information necessary for the feature may be transmitted.

Providers apply their own terms, retention, and data-use rules. AI-provider use of submitted content can depend on the account and service tier. Do not assume requests stay solely on Meyaya’s server, or that deleting our copy deletes a provider’s copy.

Outputs may be visible to other members in the Discord channel. Server managers may access authorized server features and associated records. The operator and authorized support personnel may access data as needed for administration, support, security, or data requests. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to address misuse, protect rights, or secure the service.

05Storage & retention

  • Explicit request logs: the application is configured with a seven-day retention window for captured requests and replies. Periodic cleanup removes expired records; outages or cleanup failures can delay removal.
  • Temporary context and caches: recent conversation state, cooldowns, downloaded media, and processing buffers remain for the relevant cache or session lifetime. Duration varies by feature.
  • Persistent records: memories, game progress, social statistics, and server settings do not share a single automatic expiry. They are retained for the continuing feature or until removed through available controls, a verified request, or administrative cleanup.
  • Operational records: diagnostics and any backup copies may have separate retention schedules. They are retained as needed for troubleshooting, recovery, safety, or legal obligations. Contact us for details relevant to your request.

Removing Meyaya from a server or deleting a Discord message does not automatically erase all stored records. Deleting a memory does not automatically delete game records, request logs, or Discord messages. Provider records and copies held by other Discord users are outside Meyaya’s direct control.

06Voice participation

When enabled and connected, voice features process channel audio according to the listening controls and send relevant audio to Google’s live AI service. The voice implementation uses transient processing queues rather than a dedicated permanent voice-recording archive; related text, context, or diagnostics may still be processed or retained as described above.

Before enabling voice features, tell participants that an AI bot can process their speech and obtain any permission required for your setting. If you do not want your audio processed, do not speak while Meyaya is listening, leave the channel, or ask an authorized user or server manager to disable listening or disconnect the bot.

07Your choices & data requests

Use /memories to inspect the memories available through that command. You may ask Meyaya to forget a specific fact, but AI interpretation is not a guaranteed full-data deletion method. For a reliable access, correction, or deletion request, contact the operator below.

You may request access to your information, correction of inaccurate records, deletion, or information about retention and recipients. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, and a complaint to the relevant data-protection authority.

We will verify requests as necessary, explain any limits, and respond within the period required by applicable law. Some records may need to be retained for security, legal obligations, or others’ rights. We may redact or disassociate your information from a shared record rather than disclose another person’s data. Stopping use does not itself submit a deletion request.

08Security & international processing

Information may be processed in countries where the operator and providers run their systems, which may differ from your country. Applicable safeguards and rights depend on the processing, service, and law.

We take reasonable measures to limit operational access and protect information, but no online service or transmission is completely secure. Report suspected exposure or misuse through the contact below.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing may be necessary to provide requested features, for legitimate interests such as security and reliability, to comply with legal obligations, or based on consent where required. The appropriate basis and your rights depend on the processing and your location.

09Age requirements

Meyaya is intended for people who meet Discord’s minimum age requirement and the applicable minimum age in their country. It is not intended for children below that age. Contact us if you believe a person below the permitted age has supplied personal information so we can review and address it.

10This website

These pages contain no operator-added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, forms, or cookies. When published on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes hosting and security information, including visitor IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement. Support and provider links lead to separate services with their own practices.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate bot or support channel where required. This version takes effect when published.
